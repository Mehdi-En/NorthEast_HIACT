# Public-data summary output

`public_energy_summary_2024.csv` selects the eight 2024 rows from `data/derived/study_area_energy_2005_2024.csv`: two study areas and four all-fuel measures. This is a historical summary of the openly published DESNZ statistics, not a hydrogen-model output. Its construction is explained in `SYNTHESIS.md`.

Energy is supplied in ktoe and GWh. GWh = ktoe × 11.63. The industrial/commercial/other measure includes more than industrial process heat. Regional totals include all listed authorities and must not be added to their constituent authority rows.

No paper demand projections, named-site results, calibrated allocations, cost comparisons, uncertainty results or decision thresholds are included in this output.
